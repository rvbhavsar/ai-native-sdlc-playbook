# Project or product intent

Use for a new product, bounded project, major initiative, or epic containing multiple coordinated outcomes. Split the work if independent outcomes could be approved and delivered separately.

## High-leverage questions

Ask only the unresolved questions with the greatest decision impact:

- What changed or created urgency, and what evidence supports the opportunity?
- Which primary user or stakeholder experiences the problem? Who buys, approves, operates, or is indirectly affected?
- What business or user outcome must be true at the end? What is the current baseline?
- What is the smallest coherent first boundary? Which adjacent outcomes are explicitly excluded?
- Which existing process, product, customer promise, or behavior must remain unchanged?
- What observable measure or decision test proves success, and over what useful horizon?
- Which systems, teams, policies, data, vendors, or permissions does this depend on?
- What would make the project unsafe, nonviable, or not worth continuing?
- Who owns the final intent decision and who must be consulted?

## Required structure

Use `assets/templates/project-intent.md`. Preserve:

- purpose and trigger;
- target users and stakeholders;
- current state and evidence;
- desired outcome and value;
- scope, non-goals, and unchanged behavior;
- success measures;
- constraints and guardrails;
- dependencies, assumptions, and risks;
- unresolved decisions and human approval.

Avoid roadmap details, architecture, task decomposition, staffing plans, or estimates unless they are fixed business constraints.

## Specific failure modes

- A mission statement with no bounded deliverable.
- A solution or platform choice without a validated problem.
- Several products hidden inside one “project.”
- Success defined only as shipping.
- Stakeholders listed without decision or impact roles.
- Timeline treated as evidence of value.
