# Initiative Workspace

Use this folder as the starting point for a project/product, feature, improvement, or bug.

## Sequence

1. Complete and approve [`intent.md`](intent.md).
2. Complete and approve [`spec.md`](spec.md) against that exact intent version.
3. Complete and approve [`plan.md`](plan.md) against that exact specification and repository revision.

Do not treat the placeholder content as decided. Replace it through the corresponding agentic workshop, preserve assumptions and risks, and record explicit human approval at every gate.
